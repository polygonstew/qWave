A user asked an AI assistant a question. Two responses were generated. Read both carefully, select the one you think is better, then explain your reasoning in the text box below. Be specific — vague answers like "it was clearer" won't cut it.

User's Question

"Can you explain what inflation is and how it affects everyday people?"

✓
RESPONSE A

Inflation is when prices go up over time. It makes things more expensive so your money doesn't go as far. For example, if a gallon of milk costs $3 today and inflation is 5%, it'll cost $3.15 next year. This hurts people on fixed incomes the most because their income doesn't rise with prices. It also affects savings — money sitting in a low-interest account loses purchasing power. Governments try to control inflation using interest rates.

✓
RESPONSE B

Inflation is a complex macroeconomic phenomenon characterized by a sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services in an economy over a period of time. When the general price level rises, each unit of currency buys fewer goods and services. Consequently, inflation reflects a reduction in the purchasing power per unit of money. It is measured using indexes such as the CPI.
